Privacy Policy — Anas English Agent

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This application (“Anas English Agent”) is a personal publishing tool operated by Anas, an English teacher. It posts educational content to LinkedIn on behalf of its owner only.

What the application does

  • Connects to LinkedIn using OAuth 2.0 with the owner’s explicit authorization.
  • Publishes posts (text and images) prepared by the owner or by his own AI assistant.
  • Reads the owner’s basic profile information (name, profile photo, email) to confirm identity.

What the application does NOT do

  • It does not collect, sell, or share data of any other LinkedIn member.
  • It does not access private messages of any user.
  • It does not run automated actions on accounts other than the owner’s own account.

Data storage

The OAuth access token is stored locally on the owner’s personal computer and is never transmitted to any third-party server. No other personal data is stored.

Data sharing

No data is shared with third parties. Content is sent only to LinkedIn’s official API in order to publish the owner’s own posts.

Revoking access

The owner may revoke access at any time from LinkedIn: Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Permitted services.
